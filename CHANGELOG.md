# Vanity

## v0.2.0
- **14 backgrounds**, picked from swatches: Midnight, Class color, Faction, Ember, Frost, Forest, Void, Gilded, Sunset, Slate, Pure black, Studio white, Green screen, and The world.
- **Settings panel** (`/vanity settings` or the Settings button): background, brightness, floor glow, darkened edges, camera framing (full body, waist up, bust, portrait), turntable and speed, stats panel, slot names, hints, character-window button.
- **AFK screen:** go AFK and Vanity takes over with your character slowly turning, a timer, a clock and your item level. It cycles poses (sit, wave, laugh, dance ...). Move or press a key to leave. `/vanity afk` previews it. Options for turning it off, turning off poses, and a 24-hour clock.

## v0.1.1
- New stage look: dark ceiling, a floor glow in your class color, darkened edges. "Class color" tints the whole stage.
- Enchant warnings only appear from level 40.

## v0.1.0
- First release: full-screen rotatable model, gear with item levels, missing enchant and gem warnings, durability, stats, photo studio, target viewer.
